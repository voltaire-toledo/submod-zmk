## Behavior safety assessment

`esc_caps` is an exclusive `zmk,behavior-hold-tap` node with two binding cells,
`flavor = "tap-preferred"`, `tapping-term-ms = <600>`, `quick-tap-ms = <200>`,
and `bindings = <&kp>, <&kp>`.

- ZMK's local hold-tap implementation maps binding index `0` to the hold
  behavior and index `1` to the tap behavior. Therefore `&esc_caps CLCK ESC`
  means hold Caps Lock and tap Escape.
- ZMK's `tap-preferred` flavor decides hold only after the 600 ms term; another
  key pressed before then does not force a Caps Lock toggle. Releasing before
  the hold decision emits Escape.
- The key-toggle behavior toggles on press and has an empty release handler.
  A decided hold consequently toggles Caps Lock once; release does not emit a
  second toggle or Escape.
- `quick-tap-ms = <200>` forces a rapid repeated activation to the tap path,
  protecting normal repeated Escape use.

This establishes the intended static behavior. Hardware checks for tap, long
hold, release, repeat, and interruption remain required before QA pass.
