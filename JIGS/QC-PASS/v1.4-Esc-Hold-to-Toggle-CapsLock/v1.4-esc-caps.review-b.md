## Behavior and timing assessment

The repository's hold-tap implementation assigns `bindings[0]` to the hold
behavior and `bindings[1]` to the tap behavior. Therefore
`&esc_caps CLCK ESC` means hold Caps Lock and tap Escape, not the reverse.
The runtime also uses `tapping-term-ms` as the hold decision deadline. The
requested hardware assertion must consequently use a hold *longer than*
600 ms; a tap/release before that deadline emits Escape. `tap-preferred`
resolves an interruption as a tap, and the 200 ms quick-tap window preserves
rapid repeated Escape taps as taps. No existing shared behavior was altered.
